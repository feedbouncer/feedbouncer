A rule that goes over a limit is rejected when it is saved.

A rule also has at most one of each [step](./actions.mdx), and a reply template is at most 2,000 characters.

## Per brand

- 200 rules per brand.
- 50 own domains per brand.
- 20 rule change proposals waiting for approval per brand.

A rule evaluated against a comment also has a budget of {reference.rules.ruleBudgetMs} ms. See [Precedence](./precedence.md#the-time-budget).
