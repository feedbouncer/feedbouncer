# Roadmap

This shows intent, not a commitment.

**Last updated:** 2026-10-23

Items describe what you get, not when you get it.

## Now

- **Free ad comment scan**: Check the comments on your ads for spam, scams and abuse without setting anything up first.

## Next

- **Threads comments**: Moderate Threads comments in the same place as Facebook and Instagram.
- **Messenger and Instagram direct messages**: See and answer private messages next to the comments from the same people.
- **Team inbox**: Assign conversations to teammates, leave internal notes and reuse saved replies.
- **Contact profiles**: See one person's comments and messages across platforms on a single page.
- **Response-time reports**: See how quickly your team answers customers.
- **AI reply drafts in your voice**: Get suggested replies written in your brand's voice, using a model you choose.

## Later

- **Agency client workspaces**: Manage many clients from one login, each with its own rules and connections.
- **White-label reports**: Send clients reports that carry your own logo and domain.
- **Comment insight summaries**: Get a short summary of what people complain about, ask for and feel about your ads.
- **Ad performance next to moderation**: See how hidden comments line up with how each ad performs.
- **More platforms**: Moderate Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile.
- **Helpdesk handoff**: Pass a conversation to your helpdesk when it needs more than a comment reply.

## Shipped

Shipped means merged on the main branch. It reaches you in the next release, or now if you build from main.

- **Facebook Page and Instagram comment moderation**: Hide, delete or reply to comments on your Page and your Instagram account.
- **TikTok and YouTube comments**: Moderate comments on your TikTok and YouTube videos, with a daily budget for YouTube's API quota.
- **Ad comment coverage**: Moderate the comments on your ads as well as your organic posts.
- **Backfill on connect**: Existing comments are checked when you connect an account.
- **Rules**: Decide by keyword, link, author or pattern what gets hidden or flagged.
- **AI classifier**: Catch spam, scams and abuse that simple rules miss.
- **Plain-language policies**: Describe what you want moderated in your own words.
- **Review mode**: Hold borderline comments for a person to approve instead of acting on them.
- **Undo log**: Reverse any moderation action from a record of everything that was done.
- **Unified comment list**: Browse and search comments from every connected account in one list.
- **Slack and email alerts**: Hear about comments that need attention where you already work.
- **Weekly report**: Get a summary of what was moderated each week.
- **Public API and webhooks**: Connect FeedBouncer to your own tools.
- **MCP server**: Moderate from an AI assistant that supports MCP.
- **n8n node**: Use FeedBouncer in n8n workflows.
- **Text moderation endpoint**: Send any text through your rules and classifier and get an allow, review or block verdict back.
- **Data retention, export and deletion**: Choose how long data is kept, export it, or delete it.
- **Self-host Meta app guide**: Follow a step-by-step guide to set up your own Meta app.
- **Documentation site**: Read guides, concepts and the API reference.
- **Public roadmap**: See what is planned, what is coming next and what has shipped.
